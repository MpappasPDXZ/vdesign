LEGAL CONTRACT CONSTRUCTION AGREEMENT 
 THIS CONSTRUCTION AGREEMENT (this "Agreement") made this 1st day of July, 2026 by 
and between POHLAD CUSTOM HOMES, INC., a Nebraska corporation, ("Contractor"), and MATT 
AND SARAH PAPPAS  (individually and collectively, jointly and severally, "Owner"). 
 
WITNESSETH: 
 
 WHEREAS, Builder owns certain real property commonly known as 2713 SOUTH 224 
STREET, OMAHA, NE 68022 and legally described on the attached Exhibit “A” (the "Property"); and 
 
 WHEREAS, Owner desires to have Contractor construct certain improvements on the 
Property consisting of a 3231 Sq. Ft. 1-1/2 story home with a 1325 Sq. Ft. finished basement  (Total 
living Sq. Ft. 4556) and attached 1383 Sq. Ft. 4 car above grade garage with a below grade 840 Sq. 
Ft. flex core space  (the "Improvements"). 
 
AGREEMENT: 
 
 NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants herein contained, and for other 
good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which is hereby acknowledged, the 
parties hereto agree as follows: 
 
 1. Recitals.  The above recitals are hereby incorporated into the Agreement. 
 
2. Scope of Construction Work.  The “Work” shall consist of the construction of an 
approximately 3231 square foot 1-1/5 story single-family residence with a basement, 1325 Sq. Ft.   4 
car attached garage, and a 840 Sq. Ft. flex core below grade room, concrete work and utility 
connections on the Property, all as more particularly described in the Contract Documents (as 
defined in paragraph 8, below). Owner represents that the Property is owned by Owner free and 
clear of all liens and encumbrances. 
 
3. Contractor to Perform Work.  The Contractor will perform all of the Work required 
of the Contractor by the Contract Documents and, without limitation of the foregoing, will furnish all 
skill, labor, materials, supplies, equipment, transportation, services, and other facilities and things 
necessary or proper for the carrying out and completion of the Work in substantial compliance with 
the Contract Documents.  It is expressly understood and agreed that Contractor shall have no 
responsibility for any item of construction which is not specifically included in the Work, as 
described above. 
 
 4. Owner's Cooperation.  Owner agrees to cooperate with Contractor and shall 
exercise Owner’s best efforts to enable Contractor to perform the Work as required under this 
Agreement.  Owner shall not take any action which will interfere with the timely completion of the 
Work.

 5. Subcontractors and Subcontractors' Work.  All portions of the Work not performed 
by Contractor’s own forces ("Subcontractors' Work") shall be performed under subcontract or other 
agreement between Contractor and the subcontractor (each, a “Subcontract”) with the 
subcontractor providing such Subcontractors' Work (each, a "Subcontractor"). Contractor shall 
supervise all Subcontractors performing Subcontractors' Work.  At Owner's request, Contractor will 
provide to Owner a list of all Subcontractors performing any portions of the Work. 
 
6. Mechanics' Lien Rights.  Owner will provide such information to Contractor as may 
be necessary or appropriate for Contractor to evaluate, give notice of or enforce mechanics’ lien 
rights, and otherwise carry out Contractor’s obligations hereunder.  The Contractor shall be entitled 
to rely on such information without further investigation.  Contractor will provide lien wavers from 
all Subcontractors if requested.  
 
7. Permits.  The Contractor shall obtain all the permits for the Work to be performed.  
The Contractor shall pay for any necessary permits, approvals, easements, assessments and 
charges required for construction, use or occupancy.  If Contractor cannot obtain a building permit 
within twenty one (21) days of the effective date of this Agreement, then Contractor, at its sole 
option, may terminate this Agreement. 
 
8. Contract Documents. The “Contract Documents” consist of this Agreement, the 
Drawings, Specifications and other documents, if any, described on Exhibit "B" attached hereto, 
and all written Change Orders (as defined below) issued and signed subsequent thereto in the 
manner authorized herein.  All of the above-described documents form the Contract Documents 
and all are as fully a part of this Agreement as if attached to this Agreement or set forth herein. This 
Agreement and the other Contract Documents represent the entire and integrated agreement 
between the parties hereto and supersede all prior negotiations, representations, or agreements, 
either written or oral, or both. 
 
9. Change Orders.  The Owner may order changes in the Work consisting of 
additions, deletions or modifications.  Such changes in work shall be authorized by written 
agreement signed by the Owner and Contractor ("Change Orders").  The Cost of Work and Date 
of completion may be affected based on change orders. The cost or credit to the Owner from a 
change in the Work shall be determined by Contractor. 
 
10. Commencement of Construction.  The Contractor shall commence the Work upon 
fourteen (14) days from the date of this Agreement (the "Commencement Date").  Prior to the 
commencement of the Work, Owner shall provide a clear; accessible building site, correctly zoned 
for the Improvements, and contractor shall identify the boundaries of the Property by stakes at all 
corners. 
 
11. Completion of Improvements.  Subject to extension by mutual agreement of Owner 
and Contractor, or delays caused by Force Majeure (as hereinafter defined), Contractor shall 
achieve Substantial Completion of the Improvements within ONE YEAR (12) months from the date 
on which Contractor commences the digging of the foundation.  As used herein, “Substantial 
Completion” shall mean that stage in the progress of the Work when the Work or designated portion 
thereof is sufficiently complete in substantial accordance with the Contract Documents so that the 
Owner can reasonably occupy the Improvements.  As used herein, the term “Force Majeure” shall 
mean matters beyond the reasonable control of the Contractor, such as fire or casualty, war, act of 
god, labor disputes, unknown subterranean conditions, environmental problems, unusually rainy or 
wet weather patterns, unusual delay in deliveries, or actions or neglect of the Owner.  
 
12. Contract Price.  The Owner shall pay the Contractor in current funds for the 
Contractor's performance of the Contract.

 13. Cost of Work.  The term "Cost of Work" shall mean costs necessarily incurred by 
the Contractor in the proper performance of the Work.  Such costs shall be at rates not higher than 
the standard price paid in Omaha, Nebraska and surrounding areas.  The Cost of Work shall include 
only the items set forth in this paragraph 13.  
 
(a) Wages of construction workers directly employed by the Contractor to 
perform the construction of the Work, including welfare, unemployment 
compensation, social security and other benefits. 
 
(B) Cost, including transportation, of materials and equipment incorporated or 
to be incorporated in the completed construction. 
 
(c) Payments made by the Contractor to Subcontractors in accordance with 
the requirements of the subcontracts. 
 
(d) Cost of all materials, temporary facilities, equipment and hand tools not 
customarily owned by the construction workers, which are provided by the 
Contractor at the site and fully consumed in the performance of the Work. 
 
(e) Reasonable rental costs for necessary temporary facilities, machinery, 
equipment, and hand tools used at the site of the Work, whether rented 
from the Contractor or others. 
 
(f) That portion directly attributable to this Agreement for premiums for 
insurance. 
 
(g) Losses and expenses not compensated by insurance or otherwise, 
sustained by the Contractor in connection with the Work, provided they 
have resulted from causes other than the fault or neglect of the Contractor. 
 
(h) Cost of removal of debris from the site. 
 
(i) Costs incurred in taking action to prevent threatened damages, injury or 
loss in case of an emergency affecting the safety of persons and property. 
 
(j) Costs incurred for all building permits and inspections. 
 
(k) The deductible for any claim made under a builder's risk insurance policy. 
 
(l) Other cost incurred in the performance of the Work if and to the extent 
approved in advance in writing by the Owner. 
 
 14. Payment for Cost of Work.  Contractor shall submit applications for payment to the 
Owner (or to the Owner’s lender, as the case may be) once a calendar month on the TENTH (10) 
day of the month.  Applications for payment shall reflect an itemized statement of the portions of

15. No Guaranteed Maximum Price.  Any cost estimates included in the Scope of Work 
or discussed during the negotiation or execution of the Agreement were estimates only, not 
guarantees or warranties of any kind, and are not binding on the Contractor.  This Agreement does 
not include a guaranteed maximum price for additional work or change orders outside Exhibit “B”. 
 
16. Accounting Records.  Contractor shall keep reasonable accounts and records as 
may be necessary for proper financial management of Contractor’s duties under this Agreement, 
and shall allow inspection of such records by Owner upon reasonable notice.   
 
17. Final Payment.  Final payment, constituting the entire unpaid balance of the 
Contract, Cost of Work, Shall be paid by Owner when the Work has been fully completed in 
substantial accordance with the Contract Documents, punch list items are complete and upon 
submission of Contractor’s final application for payment.  The making of final payment by the Owner 
shall constitute a waiver of all claims by the Owner against the Contractor except to the extent of 
Contractor’s limited warranty set forth in Section 21 below.  Owner shall not take possession of the 
Improvements until such time as all payments and other obligations owed by Owner to Contractor 
as set forth in this Agreement have been fully paid or performed by Owner. 
 
18. Punch List.  Prior to Owner's possession, Owner and Contractor shall inspect the 
Improvements and shall compile a list of items (if any) which remain to be completed or corrected.  
Contractor shall complete or correct all Punch List Items as soon as reasonably practicable after 
such inspection.   
 
19. Insurance.  Contractor shall maintain the following insurance: (a) Builder’s Risk 
insurance relating to the Work, in an amount not less than the Contract Price, (b) worker’s 
compensation coverage as and to the extent required by law, and (c) Contractor’s liability insurance 
in an amount not less than the Contract Price.  To the extent applicable, Contractor may maintain 
any insurance required hereunder by a “blanket” or other combined policy.  Contractor shall 
maintain such insurance in effect from the Commencement Date until the earlier of (a) the date of 
final payment, or (b) the date of termination of this Agreement.  Contractor shall provide to Owner 
certificates reflecting such insurance upon Owner’s request.   
 
20. Insurance Waiver.  Owner and Contractor hereby waive all rights against the other 
and any of Contractor's subcontractors, agents and employees, for damages caused by fire or 
other causes of loss to the extent covered by property insurance obtained pursuant to Section 
_19__ or otherwise in effect, except such rights, if any, as they may have to the proceeds of such 
insurance, and agree to maintain from their respective insurers endorsements to applicable policies 
of insurance permitting such waiver. 
 
21. LIMITED WARRANTY.  CONTRACTOR WARRANTS TO OWNER THAT THE 
IMPROVEMENTS SHALL BE FREE FROM MATERIAL DEFECTS OF WORKMANSHIP FOR A 
PERIOD OF ONE YEAR FROM THE DATE OF SUBSTANTIAL COMPLETION.  THIS 
WARRANTY IS LIMITED TO REPAIR OR REPLACEMENT, AT CONTRACTOR'S SOLE 
DISCRETION AND COST.  CONTRACTOR'S WARRANTY EXCLUDES DAMAGE OR DEFECT 
CAUSED BY ABUSE, IMPROPER OR INSUFFICIENT MAINTENANCE, IMPROPER 
OPERATION, OR NORMAL WEAR AND TEAR AND NORMAL USAGE.  ANY

22. Stress Cracks.  Due to the expanding and contracting of materials and the outside 
elements, light stress cracks may occur. These cracks may be up to 3/16”. Any significant cracks 
are subject to LIMITED WARRANTY. 
 
23. Sod.  Sod is alive when installed.  Contractor makes no guaranty or warranty 
regarding sod beyond installation.  Sod will be irrigated by Contractor until Owner takes possession.  
After possession, Owner is responsible for sod. 
 
24. Wallpaper.  Any wallpaper installation or other decorating is the sole responsibility 
of the Owner and shall only occur after possession. 
 
25. Building Code.  Contractor reserves the right to modify any Contract Documents 
to comply with any state or local health and building codes. 
 
26. Owner Financing.  This Agreement is contingent on Owner obtaining construction 
financing. 
 
27. Assignment of Contract Documents.  Neither party may assign its rights hereunder 
or under the Contract Documents without the prior written consent of the other party (which consent 
shall not be unreasonably conditioned, with held or delayed), and, unless specifically stated to the 
contrary in any written consent to an assignment, no assignment will release or discharge the 
assignor from any duty or responsibility under the Contract Documents.  Not withstanding the 
foregoing, it is understood that Contractor may perform some of its obligations hereunder through 
the use of various Subcontractors.  Contractor shall cause all Subcontractors performing the Work 
to be appropriately insured. 
 
28. Successors and Assigns.  Owner and Contractor each bind his, her, its, or their 
partners, successors, assigns and legal representatives to the other party hereto, his, her, its or 
their partners, successors, assigns and legal representatives in respect of all covenants, 
agreements and obligations contained in the Contract Documents.  If Owner consists of more than 
one individual or entity, the obligations of each such individual or entity shall be joint and several. 
 
29. Termination by Contractor.  Contractor may terminate this Agreement upon seven 
(7) days’ written notice to Owner if, through no act or fault of the Contractor, delays or interruptions 
of the Work (other than delays reasonably caused by weather conditions) or the Owner’s failure to 
pay amounts when due shall continue for a period in excess of thirty (30) days and are not cured 
within such seven (7) day notice period.  Upon termination pursuant to this paragraph, the 
Contractor shall be entitled to recover from the Owner payment for Work performed and materials 
and labor provided and/or acquired, and reasonable overhead and profit.  
 
30. Termination by Owner.  Owner may terminate this Agreement upon seven (7) days 
written notice to Contractor if, through no act or fault of the Owner, Contractor fails to perform 
Contractor’s Work in the manner described in this Agreement or fails to pay Sub-contractors for a 
period of thirty days and fails to cure within seven (7) days of Contractor’s receipt of such notice 
(provided, that such seven-day cure period shall be extended in the event that Contractor’s failure 
cannot reasonably be cured within such fifteen day period and Contractor commences such cure 
within such seven-day period and thereafter diligently pursues such cure to completion within a 
reasonable time).  Owner may also terminate this Agreement if the Contractor declares bankruptcy.  
In the event of Owner’s termination pursuant to this paragraph, Contractor shall be entitled to 
payment for the Work performed to date (including all contractual commitments made by the 
Contractor with respect there to, even if not yet performed) plus a re asonable allocation of 
Contractor’s overhead and profit relating to such portion of the Work.
