## Design implications (working notes)

1. **Two backyard levels:** main-level covered deck + lower walk-out patio/turf. Stairs + rail + any retaining are the hinge.  
2. **Usable rear depth ~57–60′** (east/west), tapering slightly; plan turf/patio inside that envelope and respect the **25′ rear setback** from the lot line.  
3. **Contract already funds** full sod, 5-zone irrigation, $10k landscape/retaining, and rear patio within the 2,100 sf concrete pool — backyard design should **spend those allowances intentionally**, not assume unlimited hardscape.  
4. **Turf vs sod:** specs say **Fescue sod** for the full yard. Any synthetic turf / “turf room” expansion is a **change order** relative to this contract (and is why the older `before turf and garage expansion/` set is excluded here).  
5. **Match the house:** white/light Hardi, black windows/rails/gutters, cedar warm accents, thin stone, Rustic Black roof. Deck already specified **Silver Maple** composite + black Omarail.  
6. **Service clearances:** A/C pads, downspouts, sump discharges, gas meter, sill cocks — pin from foundation / basement sheets before placing beds or turf.  
7. **View corridors:** Great room / dining slider and upper rear bedrooms overlook the yard — keep primary lawn/patio axis aligned with the deck stairs.
